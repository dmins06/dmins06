## About Me

Hi, I'm Minh (DIMINN.), a third-year Data Science and AI student at the University of Transport Ho Chi Minh City (UTH), Vietnam.

I'm interested in building data-driven systems, transforming raw data into meaningful insights, and applying machine learning to real-world problems.
